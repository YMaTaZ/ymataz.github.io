关于

本科应用化学，硕士 SUTD 人本设计（2026 届）。做 AI 产品，也做把人放进产品里的设计。

先在实验室待了四年，学怎么验证一个假设；再到设计学院，学怎么理解一个人。化学留下的，是对「证据是什么」的追问；人本设计给的，是「这对谁有意义」的视角。这两样，现在都长在工作方式里。

工作可以分成两半。为 AI 做设计——把不透明的 AI 决策，变成人看得懂、能监督的过程（EODD，发表于 Design Science Journal）。用 AI 做设计——把研究洞察快速做成可用的产品，直到被真实机构采纳（KKH PREMs Toolkit，落地于新加坡 KK 妇幼医院）。

方式是 AI 原生的：vibe coding 快速成型，AI 辅助设计统一界面，判断留给人和研究。

About

BSc in Applied Chemistry; MSc in Human-Centered Design, SUTD (Class of 2026). Designing for AI, and designing with AI.

Four years in a chemistry lab, learning to test a hypothesis; then design school, learning to understand a person. Chemistry left a habit of asking “what’s the evidence?”; human-centered design added “and who does it matter to?” Both now shape the way work gets done.

The work splits in two. Designing for AI — turning opaque AI decisions into processes people can read and oversee (EODD, published in Design Science Journal). Designing with AI — turning research insight into usable products, until adopted by a real institution (KKH PREMs Toolkit, deployed at KK Women’s and Children’s Hospital, Singapore).

The method is AI-native: vibe coding to prototype fast, AI-assisted design to hold the interface together, judgment left to people and research.
