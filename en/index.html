03

About me, and what I am still filling in

I am Yuan Ye, M.Sc. Human-Centred Design at SUTD (Singapore University of Technology and Design), 2025.09 — 2026.08. My first degree was not design. After switching, it took me a long time to understand one thing: the weight of design is not in the picture, it is in the decision.

Only one of the five projects counts as delivery inside a real organisation (KKH). I know that, so I do not write it up as "live" — it was delivered, then entered the hospital's internal handover process, carried forward by the project team inside the hospital. The other four are team research, a personal standard, a personal tool and a course project, and the book marks each one.

Three things I am still filling in: handover files (turning KKH decisions into components, variables and annotations, not just a screenshot), recomputable records (every conclusion opens onto its evidence), two versions I can present (20 minutes in Chinese, 5 minutes in English). Once those three are in place, I can say I am not someone who draws interfaces, but someone whose work can be taken over.

If you only ask one question, ask this

"Of these five projects, which one actually got blocked by someone else's constraints?" KKH is the only one. The order of this book, and everything I chose not to write, is prepared for that question.
